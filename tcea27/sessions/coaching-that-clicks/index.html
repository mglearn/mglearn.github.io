Instructional coaching

Coaching That Clicks: Small Moments, Meaningful Growth

Using Micro-Teaching, Video Reflection, and Evidence-Based Coaching

A teacher watches a recording of a lesson and discovers something surprising: the students who seemed engaged weren’t the ones doing most of the thinking. What happens next? Explore micro-teaching, a research-supported approach to instructional coaching that uses short video clips, evidence of student learning, and focused reflection to improve teaching practice. Experience a guided coaching conversation, practice giving descriptive feedback, and discover how small changes can lead to meaningful professional growth. Leave with a practical coaching protocol and a plan for helping teachers reflect on their practice without turning video into another evaluation tool.

Miguel Guhlin · TCEA · 50 minutes

Presenter-led video observation, individual reflection, nearby partner conversation, and practical application. No accounts, preparation, or participant devices required. Theater seating works.

Start with classroom evidenceGet the materials

Learning outcomes

  • Identify evidence of student learning and instructional patterns in a short teaching example.
  • Practice a structured, supportive coaching conversation using the Notice-Wonder-Next protocol.
  • Develop a practical plan for beginning a teacher-directed micro-teaching cycle.

What success looks like

  • Separate two observable classroom behaviors from interpretations.
  • Ask an evidence-connected Notice, Wonder, and Next question.
  • Choose a teacher-directed goal, one adjustment, and follow-up evidence.

Our fifty-minute route

  1. 0–5 min
    Connect

    Consider two impressions of the same lesson.

  2. 5–11 min
    Establish trust

    Teacher ownership, evidence, and EIIR.

  3. 11–20 min
    Observe

    Watch, think quietly, compare observations.

  4. 20–30 min
    Make meaning

    Practice Notice-Wonder-Next.

  5. 30–41 min
    Practice

    Model and rehearse a coaching conversation.

  6. 41–47 min
    Plan

    Choose a small micro-coaching sprint.

  7. 47–50 min
    Reflect

    Articulate, Connect, Extend.
