Project Management

Beyond the Rollout: Project Management That Works in K–12

We’ll work through a district technology challenge together, with clear examples and a few chances to talk. No preparation or project required.

Let’s make the rollout work for people.

You know the pattern: the equipment is installed, the announcement goes out, and the rollout still doesn’t work the way people need it to. Let’s look at what makes the difference. We’ll work through a shared district case and connect the decisions to device refreshes, cybersecurity rollouts, infrastructure upgrades, and AI implementation. I’ll show you how to set clear scope, bring the right people into the conversation, connect milestones, manage risks, and keep communication useful. We’ll use ADKAR and Kotter to think about resistance and lasting adoption, then choose KPIs that tell us more than whether installation is complete. You’ll have a few short chances to talk through what you’d do next. Leave with a practical framework and reusable templates for your team. No preparation or project required.

Follow the shared caseOpen presenter guide

Learning objectives

  • Define scope and identify stakeholders, decision authority, milestones, and dependencies in a district technology initiative.
  • Choose risk responses and communication that build stakeholder buy-in.
  • Distinguish delivery KPIs from indicators of use and sustained adoption.
  • Use a worked example to identify a next action and a response to resistance.

Success criteria

  • Identify a clear outcome, an exclusion, and an accountable milestone owner in the shared case.
  • Explain a risk response and a stakeholder communication choice.
  • Describe one delivery KPI and one adoption KPI with an evidence source and review point.
  • Recommend a next action that addresses a barrier and supports sustained use.

Ms. Rivera has a lesson ready, but her portable classroom still has no dependable internet. Facilities and IT are waiting on each other, and her principal needs a clear answer. We’ll work through this fictional situation together.
