Participate

Follow the model. Discuss the next decision.

I’ll walk you through the framework using Ms. Rivera’s classroom. You’ll have a few chances to discuss what you’d do next. No project, prework, or device required.

0–5 minutes

See beyond the technical rollout

Meet the shared district case. What decision is needed before anyone promises a completion date?

What is the strongest next action?

Check explanation

Bring the relevant owners together, identify what each team needs, name who can resolve the dependency, and agree an interim instructional plan and a dependable next update. The case does not provide evidence for a completion date.

5–15 minutes

Model scope, stakeholders, and milestones

I’ll show you the charter and milestones. Which boundary or stakeholder would you ask about?

Model a boundary

In scope: restore and verify usable service in the portable classroom.

Out of scope: redesign every campus network.

Discuss the model

I’ll show you the charter. Which outcome, exclusion, or stakeholder would you ask about? Compare your thinking with a neighbor.

15–27 minutes

Teach dependencies, risks, and communication

Let’s follow the handoff, risk response, and update. Who should act next, and why?

Who needs what before we can move?

Before ______ can happen, ______ must be ready.

______ owns the handoff and will provide ______.

If ______ happens, we will escalate to ______.

Let’s walk through the handoff and risk example. In a brief neighbor exchange, choose who should act next and what the update should say. Distinguish an existing issue from a possible future risk.

27–40 minutes

Lead change and measure what works

What tells us it’s installed? What tells us people can use it? Let’s compare the KPIs and discuss a support response.

Which statement points to stronger acceptance evidence?

Check explanation

The second statement adds agreed tests and actual use. Verify the evidence and the acceptance owner. Delivery can be necessary without proving adoption or usefulness. Choose one delivery indicator and one use/adoption indicator; set baselines and targets from local evidence.

Neighbor prompt: What does the principal need in the next update? Include status, blocker, owner, next decision, and the next check.

What does Ms. Rivera need to make this work?

Prosci’s ADKAR model names Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, and Reinforcement. Use questions about understanding, concerns, know-how, actual performance, and continuing support. A technical or structural barrier may need a different response from training.

Apply the same framework across initiatives

Device refresh

Who verifies readiness before devices reach classrooms?

Delivery KPI: Share of scheduled devices configured and accepted

Adoption KPI: Share of intended classrooms completing the agreed learning workflow

Cybersecurity rollout

How will staff receive support when the new sign-in step fails?

Delivery KPI: Share of in-scope accounts enrolled in the approved sign-in process

Adoption KPI: Successful use of that process and unresolved access barriers

Infrastructure upgrade

Who accepts the service at the classroom handoff?

Delivery KPI: Share of planned locations passing agreed service tests

Adoption KPI: Successful classroom use during the intended teaching workflow

AI implementation

Who approves the pilot scope and listens to staff concerns?

Delivery KPI: Readiness of approved pilot access, guidance, and support

Adoption KPI: Observed use of the approved workflow and unresolved support needs

Illustrative indicators, not district results or prescribed targets. Define each measure, baseline, target, source, owner, and review point locally.

40–47 minutes

Discuss the next decision

Here’s our completed framework. What would you do next, who should act, and what evidence would you check?

Completed framework for the shared case

Outcome and boundary
Verify usable service in the portable classroom; exclude a district-wide network redesign.
Stakeholders and milestone
The principal sponsors the decision, facilities and IT name handoff owners, and the teacher checks classroom usability. Agree the service test after the facilities-to-IT handoff.
Risk and communication
If the prerequisite is unverified at the next check, the project lead escalates to the sponsor. Send the principal a status, blocker, owner, decision request, and next-check update.
Delivery and adoption KPIs
Record agreed service-test pass results and successful classroom use. Establish baselines and targets before acceptance; IT supplies test evidence and the teacher confirms use at the review.
Change support
Ask about the teacher’s concern, check whether the barrier is access or know-how, provide the appropriate fix or guided practice, and schedule a follow-up.

Here’s how I’d put the plan together. Think quietly for one minute, compare next actions with a neighbor for two minutes, then we’ll compare ideas and I’ll explain the next action I’d recommend. Optional notes are for your own use; no plan is submitted.
