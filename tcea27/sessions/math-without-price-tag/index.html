Featured session

High-Impact Teaching Math Tools Without the Price Tag

50 minutes · Presenter-led demonstrations and structured participation · K–12 mathematics educators and coaches

Make student thinking visible.

Math instruction shouldn’t require another subscription. Experience free digital math tools paired with research-informed instructional strategies that make student thinking visible. Through presenter-led demonstrations and quick audience challenges, explore Digital Math Tools for number concepts, GraphSplat for data analysis and mathematical reasoning, and Quiz and Flashcard Studio for retrieval practice. See how modeling, argumentation, feedback, and problem-solving work together. Leave with TEKS-connected lessons, reusable activities, and practical ideas you can use without purchasing another tool.

Start the learning experience →Open presenter guide

What you will be able to do

  • Identify free browser-based tools that support mathematics instruction.
  • Experience research-informed instructional strategies and discuss their purpose.
  • Use manipulatives, graphs, and recall checks to make mathematical thinking visible.
  • Plan a short classroom activity with a goal, strategy, tool, and evidence of learning.
  • Locate and adapt TEKS-connected lesson resources.

Success criteria

  • Explain how a bar-chart baseline changes a visual comparison using values and units.
  • Choose a representation that fits a mathematical question and explain the match.
  • Outline a short lesson with a learning goal, strategy, tool, and observable evidence of understanding.
