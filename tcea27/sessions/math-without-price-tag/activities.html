A routine you can reuse

  1. State the learning intention and describe a successful explanation.
  2. Show a representation and ask for a prediction.
  3. Model a solution, including why each step makes sense.
  4. Contrast a common misconception; provide one corrective cue.
  5. Invite a revised explanation and a new example.
