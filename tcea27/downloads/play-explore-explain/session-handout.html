Our workshop

Play, Explore, Explain: Cooperative Learning in Action

Smartphone, tablet, or laptop; one shared device per small group is enough.

Our fifty-minute route

0–9: Play a short concept Bingo game

9–18: Notice what makes the learning cooperative

18–34: Explore two activities with your group

34–42: Share what you discovered

42–47: Make one activity work for your classroom

47–50: Explain what you’ll take with you

Six tools, clear purposes
