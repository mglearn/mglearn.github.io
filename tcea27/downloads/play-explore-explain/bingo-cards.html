Concept Bingo · paper cards and clue guide

Read, print, or adapt this resource.

How we’ll play

Use one card per person or group. Read a clue, think quietly, compare reasons, and mark the matching term. Explain a row before claiming it. Shared reasoning matters more than speed.

Clues and explanation key

1. You close your notes and try to recall the idea before looking back. Answer: Retrieval practice

2. A classmate explains why the answer makes sense, not just which option to click. Answer: Peer explanation

3. You point to a detail, example, or result that supports your claim. Answer: Evidence

4. A useful cue helps you correct a misconception and try again. Answer: Feedback

5. You describe how your thinking changed after the activity. Answer: Reflection

6. Your group works toward one product that needs everyone’s contribution. Answer: Shared goal

7. You use what learners say or do to decide what teaching comes next. Answer: Formative assessment

8. You connect a new idea to an example and explain how they relate. Answer: Elaboration

Teacher setup
