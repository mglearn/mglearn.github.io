Our group exploration sheet

Read, print, or adapt this resource.

Choose two tools

Round one: Bingo, Meme Puzzle, or Story Wheel.

Round two: Quiz and Flashcard Studio, Fortune Wheel, or Dicebreaker Creator.

Give everyone a way in

Navigator: Open the activity and invite others to direct the next action.

Explainer: Say why an answer or choice makes sense.

Questioner: Ask for evidence or an example; make space for another voice.

Connector: Record the group’s idea and connect it to a classroom goal.

Eight-minute round

Think for one minute; try for four; explain and check for two; record for one. Swap roles in round two.
