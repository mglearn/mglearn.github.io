The session

High-Impact Teaching Math Tools Without the Price Tag

Your 50-minute route

0-5 min: Which graph tells the truth?

5-15 min: Make mathematics visible

15-35 min: Investigate with GraphSplat

35-45 min: Check what sticks

45-50 min: ACE your math lesson

Participation

Think, vote, explain, check, revise. Use fingers, a neighbor explanation, paper, or optional browser controls. Stay in your seat; no account or submitted responses.

Strategy connections

Before tomorrow

Choose one mathematical goal, one strategy, and one tool serving that goal. Decide what students will explain and how you will respond to a misconception.
