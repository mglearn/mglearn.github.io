Beyond the Rollout: Project Management That Works in K–12

Presenter-led workshop

Beyond the Rollout: Project Management That Works in K–12

Follow presenter models, think briefly, discuss a decision with a neighbor, and compare with the worked example. No project, preparation, or device required.

Shared district case

A fictional portable classroom remains offline after work stalled between facilities and IT. The principal receives assurances but no dependable completion date. Use only fictional or generalized details.

50-minute route

0-5 min: See beyond the technical rollout

5-15 min: Model scope, stakeholders, and milestones

15-27 min: Teach dependencies, risks, and communication

27-40 min: Lead change and measure what works

40-47 min: Discuss the next decision

47-50 min: Review the framework and take the templates

Success criteria

Identify a clear outcome, an exclusion, and an accountable milestone owner in the shared case.

Explain a risk response and a stakeholder communication choice.

Describe one delivery KPI and one adoption KPI with an evidence source and review point.

Recommend a next action that addresses a barrier and supports sustained use.

Takeaway framework

Scope → stakeholders → milestones → risks → communication → KPIs and sustained adoption. No project or preparation required.
