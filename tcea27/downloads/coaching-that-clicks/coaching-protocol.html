Notice, Wonder, Next quick reference

Read, print, or adapt this resource.

Notice: evidence

What do you notice? What stands out? What did students do? Which patterns connect to the goal?

Wonder: inquiry

What might explain this? What surprised you? What other perspectives or evidence should we consider?

Next: action

What might you try? What support would help? How will we know whether it helped? When will we revisit?

Do

Begin with teacher reflection; recognize strengths; describe evidence; invite teacher ideas; agree on follow-up.

Avoid

Lists of mistakes, treating interpretations as facts, judging appearance, comparing teachers, unsupported confidentiality promises, or ending without a next step.

Choose the support needed

Sometimes direct information, modeling, resources, and explicit feedback help more than another question.
