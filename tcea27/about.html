About

Useful in the room. Useful tomorrow.

A growing collection of conference learning experiences and classroom resources from Miguel Guhlin.

A hub that grows with the workshops

The first published experience is High-Impact Teaching Math Tools Without the Price Tag. More sessions will appear here as their activities and resources are ready.

Our approach

Experience the strategy. Explore the tool. Apply the learning. Start with a mathematical goal, make reasoning visible, and use evidence to decide what learners need next.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, analytics, server submissions, or response collection. Presenter resources are encrypted and opened locally with a password. Passwords and decrypted notes are not saved to browser storage. The webdeck framework stores only slide position and notes text size for presenter synchronization and preferences; clear site data to remove those settings. Activity choices and ACE reflections remain in page memory; reloading or closing the page clears them. Copy or print anything you want to keep. Clipboard access is used only when you choose Copy reflection. Downloads and external links make ordinary requests to their hosts; external sites have their own policies.

Accessibility and offline use

Use native keyboard controls, visible focus, labeled graphs, text alternatives, reduced-motion support, and print styles. Explanations can be opened without JavaScript. PDFs have text and HTML companions. The extracted offline packet works without a web server; external demonstrations still need connectivity.

Research and standards

MetaX values are dated, strategy-level weighted mean effect sizes, not product ratings or promises. TEKS connections are paraphrased from the TEA Chapter 111 documents and checked October 27, 2026. Recheck sources as curricula and research evolve.
