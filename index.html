Provenance & how it's made

These resources grew out of TCEA's professional-learning work. Shared instructional scaffolding — UDL guidance, ELPS supports, and lesson-plan structure — is adapted from TCEA breakout and professional-development materials, and several suites are distilled from TCEA workshop curricula.

Works by other people are excluded from the license above and remain the property of their copyright holders. Literature activities favor public-domain texts; a few are original educational companions to works still in copyright — they reproduce no text, excerpts, or illustrations from those works and are not affiliated with or endorsed by any author, estate, or publisher. Historical journeys quote only short public-domain primary-source passages and link each source directly; scene artwork is original illustration, not reproduced historical work.

Content, code, artwork, and the multilingual translations are produced with the help of generative-AI tools and refined under Miguel Guhlin's direction and editorial review — a human stays in the loop on every decision. Translations are machine-assisted and flagged for local educator review before classroom use.

Privacy

Every activity runs entirely in the student's browser. There are no logins and no accounts, nothing is uploaded, and no student data is collected or transmitted. Progress, where an activity saves any, stays on the device.

Contact

For permissions, licensing questions, corrections, or to report an accessibility barrier, contact Miguel Guhlin at mguhlin@tcea.org. Students and families: please reach your teacher or school first — they deploy these locally and can respond fastest.
